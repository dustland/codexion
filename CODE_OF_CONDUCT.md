# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Codexion an open, welcoming, and harassment-free experience,
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience,
education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or
other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different experiences and viewpoints.
- Give specific, constructive technical feedback.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work to correct them.
- Keep discussion focused on users, safety, and project goals.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private communication, or undisclosed vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing malicious code, credentials, or data intended to harm users.
- Repeated disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers interpret and enforce this policy. They may remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report incidents privately to
the repository owner; maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project.
